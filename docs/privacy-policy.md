# Dawnly privacy policy

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

Dawnly is a free alarm helper made by Aryan Lanke. It notices when you're already up before your alarm and offers to dismiss it.

## What Dawnly reads
Only during the short watch window before your next alarm, and only for the signs you turn on:

- **Screen use:** whether the screen is on and unlocked.
- **Steps and movement:** from the phone's step counter and motion sensor, if you allow Physical activity.
- **Music:** whether something is playing.
- **Apps opened** (optional, off by default): which app comes to the front, not what you do in it, if you allow Usage access.
- **Your next alarm's time,** as Android shares it with apps.

## What Dawnly does with it
It adds these signs up on your phone to decide whether you're awake. Nothing is stored beyond the current window, except your settings.

## What leaves your phone
Nothing. Dawnly has no internet access: Android doesn't let it connect to anything. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it shares nothing with anyone, including the developer.

The one exception is Android itself. If you have backups turned on, Android may include Dawnly's settings (such as your watch window) in your phone's own backup.

## Permissions
Every permission is explained in the app before it's asked for, and you can turn any of them off in your phone's settings. Dawnly keeps working with fewer signs.

## Children
Dawnly isn't aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
