# Dawnly privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Dawnly is a free alarm helper made by Aryan Lanke. It notices when you're already up before your alarm and offers to dismiss it (or, if you choose Warn first or Right away, dismisses it for you). It also has simple reminders.

## What Dawnly reads
Only during the short watch window before your next alarm, and only for the signs you turn on:

- **Screen use:** whether the screen is on and unlocked.
- **Steps and movement:** from the phone's step counter and motion sensor, if you allow Physical activity.
- **Charger:** whether the phone was unplugged.
- **Music:** whether something is playing.
- **Apps opened** (optional, off by default): which app comes to the front, not what you do in it, if you allow Usage access. Dawnly also uses this to see which app you were in, so it can take you back there after dismissing your alarm.
- **Your next alarm's time,** as Android shares it with apps.

## What Dawnly does with it
It adds these signs up on your phone to decide whether you're awake. The readings are kept only for the current window, so Dawnly can carry on if Android restarts it.

## What Dawnly keeps on your phone
- **Your settings:** watch window, signs, sensitivity, sound, appearance.
- **Your reminders:** the name, the optional "why", the times and days, and the sound.
- **What Dawnly learned from you** (if Learning from you is on, which is the default): for the last 60 times it asked "Up already?", when it asked, how much each sign counted, and whether you answered Not yet or dismissed. From these it works out how cautious to be and your usual wake-up times. In Settings, **More options**, you can switch Learning off (nothing new is recorded) or tap **Start over** to erase it.
- **Small helpers:** the alarm Dawnly is set to watch, and which kind of dismiss request your Clock app accepts.

## What leaves your phone
Nothing. Dawnly has no internet access: Android doesn't let it connect to anything. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it shares nothing with anyone, including the developer.

Two things to know:
- **Feedback** in Settings opens your own email app with a draft addressed to the developer. The draft includes the Dawnly version, your phone's make and model, and the Android version. Nothing is sent unless you press send.
- **Android backups.** If you have backups turned on, Android may include Dawnly's settings, reminders and learned history in your phone's own backup. Readings and anything specific to one phone are left out.

## Permissions
Every permission is explained in the app before it's asked for, and you can turn any of them off in your phone's settings. Dawnly keeps working with fewer signs.

## Children
Dawnly isn't aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
